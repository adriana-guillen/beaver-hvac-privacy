Privacy Policy

Beaver HVAC Scope Builder · Android app · Effective date: 16 October 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the Android app Beaver HVAC Scope Builder (package com.beavertrades.hvacscopebuilder), published by Adriana Guillén, operating under the brand Beaver Trades Co. “Beaver Trades Co.” is the brand name used for the app.

The app is a decision-support tool for residential HVAC replacement contractors and their technicians. It does not require an account.

Short version

  • The surveys, notes, customer or job details, photos and PDF reports you create stay on your phone. The app does not upload them and we do not receive them.
  • There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service in the app.
  • Subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. The app only asks Google Play whether you have an active subscription. We never see your payment details.
  • Your survey data and reports leave your phone only when you open or share a report using another app. Separately, the Google Play subscription check goes to Google Play, and tapping a source or subscription link opens a website or Google Play outside this app.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes everything the app stores.

What the app stores on your phone

The app stores the following in its private storage on your phone. Other apps cannot read it. The app turns off Android app backup and sets Android 12+ data-extraction rules that exclude its files from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. We have not tested every phone maker’s own transfer or backup tool, so we cannot guarantee how a particular manufacturer’s tool treats this data.

  • Survey data: your answers, notes, scope selections, and any customer name, address or job reference you type in.
  • Photos you add to a survey, taken with your camera app or chosen with Android’s photo picker.
  • PDF reports that you generate.
  • A small subscription record: the status Google Play last reported, the time of the last successful check, and bookkeeping that confirms a purchase was acknowledged to Google Play. The purchase token is kept only until Google Play confirms the acknowledgement; afterwards only a one-way hash of it is kept.
  • A backup copy of a survey made once when the app upgrades an older survey to a newer format.

If you enter information about your customers, you are responsible for handling it according to your own obligations to them. The app has no way to see it.

What we collect

We do not collect your survey data, photos, reports, location, contacts, device identifiers or advertising ID. The app’s own code makes no network requests. We do not operate a server for this app, and no verification server is involved in checking your subscription.

Google Play Billing and network use

The version of the app distributed through Google Play includes Google’s Play Billing Library. It lets the app use the internet connection and the Google Play service on your phone to show the subscription offer, start a purchase, restore a purchase, and check whether you hold an active subscription. This communication is between Google Play on your phone and Google. We, the developer, do not receive it and run no server for it. For this reason the app declares the Android permissions INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and com.android.vending.BILLING.

What the app receives from Google Play: a purchase token, the product ID, the purchase state (for example purchased or pending), whether the purchase was acknowledged, whether it renews automatically, and the offer and price text Google Play shows for you. The app never receives your card details, name or email address.

Google Play handles your payment, free-trial eligibility, renewal and cancellation under Google’s own terms and privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). Google Play processes your purchase and subscription records itself. Because the Billing Library exchanges purchase information with Google Play, our Google Play Data safety summary lists purchase history, collected only to provide the subscription feature and not shared by us. The app does not track trial dates and does not show renewal or trial-end dates; those are shown in Google Play.

To let you start a new survey without a signal, the app keeps on your phone the time of its last successful check with Google Play. After a successful check you can start new surveys offline for up to seven days; after that, the app needs to reach Google Play again. Surveys and reports you already have always stay available.

Photos and metadata

The app opens your phone’s camera app to take a photo, or Android’s photo picker to choose one; the app does not ask for camera or storage permission. Photos are saved with the survey in the app’s private storage.

The app saves the photo file as your camera app or the photo source provides it, and it does not read or remove location data. If your camera app saves the location in photos, or the photo you choose already contains location data, that data stays inside the stored photo file. The app does not add photo metadata to PDF reports: it draws each photo onto the report page.

Sharing and exports

A PDF report leaves the app’s private storage only when you ask. Preview opens the report in an external PDF viewer that you choose, and Android gives that viewer temporary read access to that report file; that app then handles it under its own policies. Share is a separate transfer: it opens Android’s share sheet, and you choose the receiving app or person. A report contains what is in the report, including any job, customer and photo content you entered. That app, and anyone you send the report to, handles it under their own policies; the developer has no access to it.

There is no other export of survey data. Source links in the report and app, and the link to manage your subscription, open a website or Google Play outside the app; the app does not send any survey data with them, and those sites have their own policies and may log your visit.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Everything stays on your phone until you remove it. The app has no account, so there is nothing for us to delete on a server.

  • You can delete an individual photo inside a survey.
  • Version 1 does not include a button to delete a whole survey or report. To remove all surveys, photos, reports and the subscription record, uninstall the app, or open Android Settings → Apps → the app → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Deleting the app does not delete copies of reports you shared with other apps or people.

Because the app is configured to exclude its data from backup and device transfer, a reinstalled app or a new phone is expected to start empty, and we cannot restore your surveys. Export or keep your own copies of reports you need.

Security

Data is kept in the app’s private storage, which Android protects from other apps. The app does not add its own encryption on top of Android’s device protections, so keeping your phone locked and updated is part of protecting your data. Communication with Google Play is handled by Google’s service.

Children

The app is made for contractors and technicians and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data handling changes, this page will be updated and the effective date below will change.
